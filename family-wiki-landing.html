The problem

Right now you're doing a job
no one knows exists.

🧠
You're the only one who knows
Ava's meds need food first. Luca can't be put on the spot. Mia needs 30 minutes alone before she can talk. You know all of it. It lives exactly nowhere else.
🔄
You explain it over and over
To the babysitter. To your husband. To your mom when she visits. To the sub. The same information, leaving your mouth, again and again, forever.
💸
It costs you spoons every day
The mental load of holding all of this — every rule, every why, every person's operating manual — is exhausting. And invisible. Nobody sees it but you.
😶
Nothing is written down
Not because you're disorganized. Because there was never a tool that made it easy enough to be worth doing. Until now.
"What happens to your family
when you have a bad brain day?"
How it works

You talk.
Claude builds the wiki.

No forms. No systems. One conversation per page — ask about The Kids, your partner, how your house runs, your own brain. Start with the defaults or build whatever your family actually needs.

01
Claude asks. You talk.
Specific, scenario-based questions — not open-ended overviews. "Tell me about a Monday when dinner went wrong." You just answer.
02
It tags what it hears.
Claude reflects back exactly what it heard and tags it in real time: #morning #logistics #luca. You confirm, correct, or add more.
03
A file saves to your Drive.
Plain markdown. iCloud or Google Drive. Not locked in an app. Readable by anyone, anywhere, forever.
04
Your widgets get smarter.
Dinner Decider knows Luca's safe foods. School Scramble knows Ava's meds. Everything connects to everything.
05
The community fills the gaps.
Other families share the questions that unlocked something — and what their wiki looked like after. Build any page you need.
